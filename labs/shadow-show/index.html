The big idea
  1. A shadow play needs a source of light, puppets that block the light, and a screen that lets some light through.
  2. Puppets closer to the light make bigger shadows; closer to the screen, smaller.
  3. Turning a puppet changes the shape of its shadow — edge-on, a bird is just a line.
