The big idea
  1. Light travels in straight lines. It cannot bend round a corner to reach a hole.
  2. A shadow forms when an object blocks light — and it has the same shape as the object.
  3. No light through: a dark shadow. Some light through: a faint shadow.
  4. Most light through: no real shadow at all, just a faint outline.
