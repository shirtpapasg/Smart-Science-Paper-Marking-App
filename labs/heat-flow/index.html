The big idea
  1. Heat flows from the hotter object to the colder one.
  2. It keeps flowing until both are at the same temperature.
  3. Then it stops — there is no difference left to drive it.
  4. A source of heat is something hotter than its surroundings.
  5. Common sources: the Sun, a stove, a flame — and our own bodies.
