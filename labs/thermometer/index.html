The big idea
  1. Your sense of touch gives a rough idea of hot or cold — never the exact temperature, and not the same answer for everyone.
  2. Temperature is a measure of how hot or cold an object is.
  3. A thermometer measures temperature accurately. Everyone who reads it properly gets the same number.
  4. The unit of temperature is degrees Celsius (°C).
  5. Read it properly: tip in the water and off the bottom, wait until the column stops changing, and read at eye level.
