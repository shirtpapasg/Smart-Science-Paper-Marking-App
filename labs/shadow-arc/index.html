The big idea
  1. The position of the source of light decides where the shadow falls.
  2. A shadow forms on the opposite side from the light, because the figure blocks the light that would have landed there.
  3. The position of the light also decides how long the shadow is.
  4. Light from the side, low down: the longest shadow. Light directly above: the shortest.
