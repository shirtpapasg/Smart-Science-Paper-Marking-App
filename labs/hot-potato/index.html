The big idea
  1. Heat is a form of energy. It is not the same thing as temperature.
  2. When an object gains heat its temperature rises. When it loses heat its temperature falls.
  3. When two objects at different temperatures touch, heat flows from the hotter one to the colder one until both are at the same temperature.
  4. The hotter object is the source of heat — and that can be the potato or the water, depending on which is hotter.
